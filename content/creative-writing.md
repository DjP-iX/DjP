## Editorial Work

{{< accordion title="Managing Editor, Grist: A Journal of the Literary Arts" >}}
I served as managing editor of [*Grist: A Journal of the Literary Arts*](https://gristjournal.com/) for issues 11–13 (2018–2020). I coordinated submissions and production with the genre editors, helping bring each annual issue into print.

{{< accordion title="Co-editor, Kentucky Writers: The Deus Loci and the Lyrical Landscape" >}}
Matthew Nickel and I co-edited this collection on Kentucky writing and its relationship to place. Des Hymnagistes Press published it in 2016. Contributors include Wendell Berry, Maurice Manning, Vivian Shipley, and H. R. Stoneback.
