## Editorial Work

{{< accordion title="Managing Editor, Grist: A Journal of the Literary Arts" >}}
Served as managing editor for Issues 11 (2018), 12 (2019), and 13 (2020) of *Grist: A Journal of the Literary Arts*, an international literary publication produced by the University of Tennessee's English Department. Responsibilities included coordinating editorial board activities, managing submission workflows, collaborating with faculty advisors, overseeing production schedules, and guiding the journal through print and digital publication processes. The role required balancing artistic vision with practical project management while mentoring undergraduate editorial staff.
{{< /accordion >}}

{{< accordion title="Editor, Kentucky Writers: The Deus Loci and the Lyrical Landscape" >}}
Edited *Kentucky Writers: The Deus Loci and the Lyrical Landscape*, a collection published by Des Hymnagistes Press in 2016. The anthology features essays and creative work exploring Kentucky's literary traditions and the relationship between writers and regional identity. Contributors include established Kentucky authors such as Wendell Berry alongside emerging voices. The editorial process involved soliciting submissions, coordinating with contributors, developmental editing, copyediting, and working with the press on production and design decisions.
{{< /accordion >}}
