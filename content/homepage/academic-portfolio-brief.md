I hold a PhD in English from the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, where I served as a postdoctoral lecturer. My scholarship focuses on Southern literature, American modernism, and regional identity. I've published essays in edited collections and peer-reviewed journals, presented at numerous national conferences, and served as President of the Elizabeth Madox Roberts Society. My academic work has been supported by the Chancellor's Fellowship, Norman J. Sanders Dissertation Fellowship, and W.E.B. Du Bois Research Fellowship.
