My creative writing practice spans poetry and fiction, exploring themes of memory, place, and the intersections of personal and historical narratives. My work has appeared in journals including *Anti-Heroin Chic*, *The Mantle*, and *The Ekphrastic Review*. I've also served as Managing Editor for *Grist: A Journal of the Literary Arts* (Issues 11-13) and edited the collection *Kentucky Writers: The Deus Loci and the Lyrical Landscape*.
